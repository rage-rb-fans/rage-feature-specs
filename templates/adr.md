# ADR-001: Decision name

## Context

Explain the decision to be made and the constraints that influence it.

## Options considered

### Option A

Describe the option, benefits, and drawbacks.

### Option B

Describe the option, benefits, and drawbacks.

## Decision

State the selected option and why it was chosen.

## Consequences

Describe what becomes easier, harder, or constrained by this decision.
